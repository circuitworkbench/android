The schematic editor has four main areas:

TOOLBAR
At the top of the screen. Contains the circuit name, Save, Undo, Redo, and the overflow menu (three dots) for less frequent actions like Save As, Rename, and Delete.

CANVAS
The main drawing area. This is where you place components, draw wires, and arrange your schematic. The canvas is infinite in all directions — pan and zoom freely. Components snap to the grid during placement and movement. Grid visibility, spacing and snapping can be configured from the settings activity.

BOTTOM/LEFT PANEL
A tabbed panel at the bottom(portrait mode) or left(landscape) of the screen with five tabs. To open any of the tabs, simply tap on the tab icon. To close or collapse the tab, tap on the icon again. The following tabs are available:

- Palette: A scrollable panel containing all available component types organised by category: Tools, Elementary Devices, Independent Sources, Dependent Sources, Meters, Semiconductors, Transmission Lines, and Subcircuits. Tap an item to arm the placement tool for that component type. Long pressing an item, will arm it in repeating mode. A '⟳' symbol is shown when the placement tool is armed in repeating mode.

- Properties: Shows the editable properties of the currently selected component or wire. To view the properties of any component, first tap the component to ensure its selected and then open the properties view by tapping tab icon. When the properties view is open with no active component or wire selection, the properties of the circuit are shown. This allows you to modify the circuit title displayed in the Netlist.
  
- Netlist: Shows the SPICE netlist that will be sent to the simulator. Updates automatically when you save the schematic or run a simulation.
  
- Problems: Shows validation errors that would prevent a simulation from running, such as unconnected pins or missing model assignments.

- Console: Displays output messages from the simulation engine. Use it to monitor the status of a running simulation.
